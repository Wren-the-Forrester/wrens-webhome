Imogen
Imogen is a scholar who inadvertently became a wandering adventurer in the pursuit of their studies. Turns out that if you end up doing enough dangerous favours for people you become the one who does the dangerous favours.

Not helping things is their fey patron, Padithas, who has much planned for them...

They are missing their left arm and right leg. They lost their arm in an "accidental" rock slide in their teenage years, and they lost their leg protecting a friend.

Species: Tiefling

Age: 20-ish

Pronouns: They/Them

Personality: Very learned in their specific studies of magicks and spells, but a bit absent-minded in other affairs. They pack-bond very quickly, and care deeply for their friends.

Colours: Dark blue, pink, and crimson.
