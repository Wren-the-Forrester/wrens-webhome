Myre
Myre's just your normal average young adult! Except she has an unhealthy fascination with insects and looks like ae's kind of rotting in the wrong light. She lives in an uncanny liminal city known as the Lost Bay, trapped in an alternate version of the 90s.
The words "polite" and "careful" aren't in her vocabulary, she flirts by bullying, and she doesn't know how to interact peacefully with anything. She is, by all accounts, shitty. 

Species: Human (mostly)

Age: 20

Pronouns: She/Her, Ae/Aer

Personality: Hides aer real emotions by shoving them down into her carapaced heart, and, when that doesn't hide them well enough, expressing them through anger and only anger. She wants so desperately to love and be loved, but can't stand being known. 

Colours: Gray, green, beige
