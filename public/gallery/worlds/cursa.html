Cursa

Cursa was a worldbuilding project that I worked on during 2019 - 2020, together with my partner at the time. It was a fantasy world where the majority of the planet was covered in a magical, deadly Forest. The stories we told in it explored how civilizations would develop in this suffocating setting. They formed tightly bound groups of society, each eking out a living in unique ways, adapting themselves to their surroundings.
