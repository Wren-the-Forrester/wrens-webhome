Bruin
Bruin is an adventurer who's scared of setting down roots. They tried to become a healer, but quickly discovered that they're much better at hurting people than they are at fixing them. They have a habit of keeping themself at a distance, afraid of getting too close and damaging those they love.

They always carry their spirit-bound sword Aico, often seeking advice from him. They hope to one day find a home for him where he can rest. (He also inexplicably has the voice of Gilbert Gotfried because the DM did it once and it stuck)

Species: Bear-flavoured weretouched

Age: 35-ish

Pronouns: They/Them but in a way where they don't really care what pronouns you use.

Personality: Gruff and quick to anger. Desperate for connection, but doesn't know how to healthily relate to people. They will always protect those friends that they do end up making, but their idea of protection isn't always helpful.

Colours: Gray, brown, and red
